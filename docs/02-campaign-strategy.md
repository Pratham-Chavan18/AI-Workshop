# Campaign Strategy

## Campaign
**AI Workshop — Campus Challenge**

### Message
> Build your first AI project in 60 minutes. One hour. One real project. Zero cost.

## Acquisition Priority

### 1. Campus Ambassadors — Primary
Recruit approximately 25 student coordinators across engineering colleges.

Target:
- 25 ambassadors × 12 valid registrations = 300 registrations

Use:
- Coding clubs
- IEEE/CSI/student chapters
- Placement groups
- Class representatives

### 2. WhatsApp Communities — Secondary
Give ambassadors short ready-to-forward copy and unique referral links.

Target:
- 100 registrations

### 3. Student Referral Loop — Growth Engine
Ask every registrant to invite three classmates immediately after registering.

Target:
- 75 registrations

### 4. Organic Social/Other — Supporting
Use LinkedIn, Instagram and student communities for credibility and additional reach.

Target:
- 25 registrations

## Total Target
| Channel | Target |
|---|---:|
| Campus ambassadors | 300 |
| WhatsApp communities | 100 |
| Student referrals | 75 |
| Organic social/other | 25 |
| **Total** | **500** |

## Budget
Recommended use of ₹2,000:
- Use as a small performance/reward pool for top campus ambassadors.
- Do not spend the full budget on broad paid ads.

## Seven-Day Cadence

### Day 1
Recruit campus ambassadors and seed the first communities.

### Day 2
Launch ambassador referral links and WhatsApp distribution.

### Day 3
Push campus-level sharing and monitor conversion.

### Day 4
Launch public leaderboard and “bring 3 friends” push.

### Day 5
Publish social proof and campus rankings.

### Day 6
Run final 48-hour registration push.

### Day 7
Final-day reminders and registration close.

## Core Growth Loop
```text
Campus Ambassador
      ↓
WhatsApp / Student Community
      ↓
Student Registration
      ↓
Unique Referral Link
      ↓
Friend Shares / Registers
      ↓
Referral Count
      ↓
Campus Leaderboard
      ↓
More Competition + Sharing
```
